About the Speaker

Professional Biography

Engr. Muhammad Ghazanfar Rehman is an accomplished Avionics Electronics Engineer, IT/ICT Professional, educator, and IEEE volunteer with more than a decade of experience in engineering leadership, professional development, and STEM education.

As the Secretary of IEEE Young Professionals Karachi, he is committed to empowering students, fresh graduates, and early-career professionals by bridging technical excellence with the workplace skills required to succeed in today's competitive professional environment.

Since beginning his IEEE volunteer journey in 2010, Engr. Ghazanfar has contributed to numerous national and international IEEE initiatives, leading technical events, educational programs, workshops, and conferences — including IEEE PCSC 2017, KSYWC 2015, and the National Flying Competition 2013 — while mentoring the next generation of engineers and innovators.
